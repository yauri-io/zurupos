## 1. The idea in one line

**Zuru is your shop's trusted record-keeper.**

*Zuru* is spelled with a Z but sounds like the Indonesian *juru*, the skilled, trusted person who does a job: *juru masak*, *juru kunci*, *juru tulis*. ZuruPOS records payments and doesn't process them, so it is the shop's *juru tulis*. The cashier sells and the barista makes the drinks; Zuru writes everything down honestly, and the owner can check it from anywhere.

The Z keeps the name ownable (*juru* is an everyday word) while still sounding familiar.

## 2. Lines

| Use | Line |
|---|---|
| Main tagline (Bahasa) | **Juru catat warungmu.** |
| Receipt footer | **Dicatat oleh Zuru** |
| Secondary, English / founder story | **The unfair advantage for small shops.** |

**About the secondary line:** in Japanese, *zurui* (ずるい) means sly or unfair, but it is also said playfully when someone has an advantage that feels unfair. We use that: chains pay for big systems with tiers and add-ons; a warung gets everything for one low flat price.

**Rule:** the "unfair" is always about **price and value**, never about money handling. Never joke about cheating near cash, voids, payments or reports. In those places Zuru means only *honest and exact*.

## 3. What we stand for

From the PRD; every page, screen and message should be consistent with these.

1. **Built for small counter-service shops, not for everyone.** Coffee shops, warung kopi, boba, bakeries.
2. **One plan, one flat price, everything included.** No tiers, no add-ons, no feature gating.
3. **Record, don't process.** Keep your QRIS sticker, EDC and cash drawer. No MDR fees, no settlement delays.
4. **The owner sees the shop from anywhere.** How much did we sell, how was it paid, what's running low, is the cash right.

## 4. Logo

**The mark:** a strip of thermal receipt paper unrolling from a roll. The strip folds into a **Z**, with torn zigzag edges. The end of the roll is the one amber accent.

**The wordmark:** `zuru` in lowercase, rounded and heavy.

| Version | Use | File |
|---|---|---|

| Simplified mark (no text lines) | under 24px: favicon 16/32 | `docs/brand/zuru-icon-simple.png` |
| Mark on amber | app launcher, apple-touch-icon, web app icons | generated by `generate_icons.py` |

| Play Store icon 512 | Google Play listing | `docs/brand/play-store-icon-512.png` |

### Rules

- **Use the mark or the wordmark alone in most places.** Together they read "Z zuru"; keep the lockup for large spaces.
- **Below 24px use the simplified mark.**
- **Receipts are black only.** The mark survives a 58mm thermal print because of its thick outline; don't add grey fills.
- **Clear space:** at least the width of the roll end on every side.
- **Don't:** recolor the paper, add shadows or gradients, rotate, put the mark on busy photos, or redraw the Z as a normal letter.

## 5. Colours

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#1A1A1A` | Outlines, text, dark backgrounds |
| **Paper** | `#F6F0E4` | Main background (receipt paper) |
| **Gula aren amber** | `#C8873A` | The one accent: app icon background, highlights, buttons with ink text |
| **Amber dark** | `#8F5B1E` | Amber for **text and links** on paper or white |

Use mostly paper and ink, with amber as the single warm accent. Avoid the corporate blue of other Indonesian POS brands.

### Contrast (WCAG)

| Pair | Ratio | OK for |
|---|---|---|
| Ink on Paper | 15.3 : 1 | all text |
| Ink on Amber | 5.8 : 1 | text and buttons (amber button, **ink** label) |
| Amber dark on Paper | 5.0 : 1 | text, links |
| Amber on Paper | 2.7 : 1 | **decoration only**, never text |
| White on Amber | 3.0 : 1 | **not for body text**; large headings only |

## 6. Type

- **Wordmark:** the custom rounded lowercase `zuru`. Don't retype it in a font.
- **Website and marketing:** a sturdy, friendly sans with good Indonesian readability. Suggestion: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (open source, designed in Jakarta). Bold for headlines, regular for body.
- **Apps:** keep the system / Nuxt UI defaults for UI text; brand shows through colour and the mark, not a custom UI font.

## 7. Voice

Zuru talks like a trusted *juru*: plain Bahasa, short sentences, a little dry humour, never salesy, never shouting.

| Instead of | Zuru says |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ Low Stock Alert! | Susu tinggal 2 liter. Besok pagi habis. |
| Revolutionize your business with our all-in-one solution! | Catat penjualan, cek stok, lihat laporan dari HP. Itu saja. |
| Upgrade to Premium to unlock reports | (never; there is one plan with everything) |
| Transaction failed. Error code 500. | Pesanan belum terkirim. Coba lagi sebentar. |

Principles:
1. **Bahasa first**, English second. Everyday words over jargon (*laporan*, not *analytics*).
2. **Say the number.** Owners want facts: Rp, liters, pieces, today.
3. **Honest about limits.** If Zuru doesn't do something (process payments, tables in MVP), say so plainly.
4. **No pricing gimmicks:** no "starting from", no "contact sales", no strikethrough prices.
